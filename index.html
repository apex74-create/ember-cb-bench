3 · Message over the house transport (MTOF)

Messages here do not ride the ordinary web pipe. They are turned into tokens by the house transport (MTOF), carried as one sealed envelope, and opened at the far end — which checks the seal by hash before showing you a word. Test traffic rides the house reserve, channel 269, kept apart from user channels.
